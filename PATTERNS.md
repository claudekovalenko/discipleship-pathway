# Patterns Through the Whole Bible

22 patterns, each named by a phrase of Scripture and traced from Genesis to Revelation. Scripture quotations are from the ESV unless marked NIV.

Patterns 1 and 2 are that focus. The rest flow from it.

# The Mature Disciple

Named in Scripture's own words: Jesus' definitions of a disciple, and the verses that use the words mature, perfect, complete, fully trained, fathers, and strong.

## At a glance

**Jesus' marks of a disciple**

1. **Abides in the Word**: “If you abide in my word, you are truly my disciples” (John 8:31)
2. **Loves others**: “By this all people will know that you are my disciples” (John 13:35)
3. **Bears fruit**: “That you bear much fruit and so prove to be my disciples” (John 15:8)
4. **Carries the cross**: “Whoever does not carry their cross and follow me cannot be my disciple” (Luke 14:27 NIV)
5. **Gives up everything**: “Those of you who do not give up everything you have cannot be my disciples” (Luke 14:33 NIV)

**Marks of maturity**

6. **Knows God deeply**: “Fathers, because you know him who is from the beginning” (1 John 2:13)
7. **Discerns good and evil**: “Solid food is for the mature” (Hebrews 5:14)
8. **Stands firm in the truth**: “So that we may no longer be children, tossed to and fro” (Ephesians 4:14)
9. **Speaks the truth in love**: “Speaking the truth in love” (Ephesians 4:15)
10. **Controls the tongue**: “Perfect, able to keep their whole body in check” (James 3:2 NIV)
11. **Endures trials**: “That you may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing” (James 1:4)
12. **Loves even enemies**: “Be perfect, therefore, as your heavenly Father is perfect” (Matthew 5:48 NIV)
13. **Obeys out of love**: “His love is made complete in us” (1 John 4:12 NIV)
14. **Keeps pressing on**: “I press on toward the goal” (Philippians 3:14 NIV)
15. **Teaches others**: “By this time you ought to be teachers” (Hebrews 5:12)
16. **Bears with the weak**: “We who are strong ought to bear with the failings of the weak” (Romans 15:1 NIV)
17. **Settled in God's will**: “Stand mature and fully assured in all the will of God” (Colossians 4:12)
18. **Becomes like Jesus**: “Everyone when he is fully trained will be like his teacher” (Luke 6:40)
19. **Ready for every good work**: “Complete, equipped for every good work” (2 Timothy 3:17)
20. **Sound and steady**: “Sound in faith, in love and in endurance” (Titus 2:2 NIV)
21. **Growing in character**: “If these qualities are yours and are increasing” (2 Peter 1:8)

## What every disciple grows toward

**Romans 8:29 (NIV)**
> “For those God foreknew he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brothers and sisters.”

**2 Corinthians 3:18 (NIV)**
> “And we all, who with unveiled faces contemplate the Lord’s glory, are being transformed into his image with ever-increasing glory, which comes from the Lord, who is the Spirit.”

**Colossians 1:28**
> “Him we proclaim, warning everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom, that we may present everyone mature in Christ.”
